**Comparison.** Neither the Prime Lease (2A) nor the Sub-Lease (2B) mentions a named insured, an insured-identity restriction, notice of cancellation, certificates, control of claims, or insurable interest. The Sub-Lease alone requires annual "documentation of loss payee" within 10 days of the Sublessor's request. That obligation runs to Bottle Stop, not to Stevens. Stevens' sworn Rog 11 requirement ("would not include any entity other than…") has no textual counterpart in L-1 to L-6. The textual requirements that Stevens' assertions do track are (i) "keep the improvements insured", which matches the Tract A/B ground, and (ii) "loss payable jointly to Lessor and Lessee", which matches the Lessee-not-a-loss-payee ground.
